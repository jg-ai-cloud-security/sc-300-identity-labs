\# Design Principles



\## Zero Trust Security

Never trust any user or device automatically. Verify every access request.



\## Verify Explicitly

Validate identity, device, location, application, and risk signals before granting access.



\## Risk-Based Access Control

Use risk signals to determine whether access should be allowed, challenged, or blocked.



\## Least Privilege Access

Provide only the minimum permissions required.



\## Continuous Risk Assessment

Continuously monitor users and sign-in activity for security threats.
