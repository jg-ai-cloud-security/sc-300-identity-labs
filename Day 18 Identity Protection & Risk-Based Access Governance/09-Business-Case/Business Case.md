\# Business Case



\## Business Problem



Compromised identities and risky sign-ins may go undetected.



\## Business Risk



\- Account compromise

\- Unauthorized access

\- Data exposure

\- Security incidents



\## Solution



Implement Identity Protection governance.



\## Benefits



\- Improved threat detection

\- Improved security posture

\- Faster remediation

\- Stronger Zero Trust security
