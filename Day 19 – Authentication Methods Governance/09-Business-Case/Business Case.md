\# Business Case



\## Business Problem



Weak authentication methods increase the risk of account compromise and unauthorized access.



\## Business Risk



\- Credential theft

\- Phishing attacks

\- Password compromise

\- Unauthorized access



\## Solution



Implement strong Authentication Methods governance.



\## Benefits



\- Improved authentication security

\- Reduced phishing risk

\- Improved identity protection

\- Stronger Zero Trust security
