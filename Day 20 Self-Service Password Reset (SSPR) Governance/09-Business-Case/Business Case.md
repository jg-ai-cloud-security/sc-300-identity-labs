\# Business Case



\## Business Problem



Users forget passwords, resulting in support calls, account lockouts, and reduced productivity.



\## Business Risk



\- Unauthorized password reset

\- Account compromise

\- Productivity loss

\- Increased helpdesk workload



\## Solution



Implement Self-Service Password Reset governance.



\## Benefits



\- Reduced helpdesk calls

\- Faster account recovery

\- Improved user productivity

\- Stronger identity security
