\# Standard Operating Procedure



\## Purpose



Perform a final Microsoft Entra ID Identity Governance Assessment.



\## Procedure



1\. Review Identity Governance controls.

2\. Review Conditional Access policies.

3\. Review Identity Protection controls.

4\. Review Authentication Governance.

5\. Review Access Governance controls.

6\. Review Privileged Access Governance.

7\. Validate Zero Trust alignment.

8\. Document findings and recommendations.



\## Expected Result



Identity governance maturity validated and governance readiness confirmed.
