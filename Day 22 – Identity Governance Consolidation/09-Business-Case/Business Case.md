\# Business Case



\## Business Problem



Identity security controls are often implemented independently, making it difficult to assess overall governance maturity.



\## Business Risk



\- Excessive permissions

\- Account compromise

\- Privileged access abuse

\- Compliance failures

\- Weak authentication controls



\## Solution



Implement a consolidated Microsoft Entra ID Identity Governance framework.



\## Benefits



\- Improved security posture

\- Better governance visibility

\- Reduced identity risk

\- Stronger compliance

\- Zero Trust alignment
