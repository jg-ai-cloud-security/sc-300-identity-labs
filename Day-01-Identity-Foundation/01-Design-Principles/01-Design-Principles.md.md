\# Design Principles — DR Taxi Identity Architecture



\## Overview

This design follows core cloud security principles to ensure secure and scalable identity management.



\## Principles Applied

\- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

\- Least Privilege

\- Identity-first security

\- Group-based access control (no direct permissions)

\- Separation of duties



\## Design Approach

Identity is used as the primary control layer to secure access to business systems and booking channels.



\## Outcome

A structured identity model that supports secure access, scalability, and future security controls.
