\## Groups — Tech Tenant



This screenshot shows the role-based security groups created to implement RBAC.



Groups were structured using a standard naming convention (DRTaxi-<Role>) to ensure clarity, scalability, and consistent access control.



These groups are used to assign access to users based on their business roles rather than assigning permissions directly.
