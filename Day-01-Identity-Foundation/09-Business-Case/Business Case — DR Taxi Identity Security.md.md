\# Business Case — DR Taxi Identity Security



\## Current Environment

Bookings are managed via:

\- WhatsApp

\- Email

\- Phone

\- Facebook

\- Calendar



\## Problem

\- No structured identity control

\- Shared account risks

\- Lack of access visibility



\## Solution

Implemented identity architecture using Microsoft Entra ID with RBAC.



\## Outcome

\- Controlled access

\- Reduced risk

\- Scalable security model



\## Future

Ready for Conditional Access and BYOD security implementation.
