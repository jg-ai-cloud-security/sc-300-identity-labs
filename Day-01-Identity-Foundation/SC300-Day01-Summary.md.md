\# SC-300 Day 01 — Delivery Summary



\## Summary

Day 01 focused on designing and implementing the identity foundation for DR Taxi.



\---



\## What Was Built

\- Identity architecture aligned to business operations

\- RBAC model using role-based security groups

\- User-to-group mapping based on real business roles

\- Access structure for multi-channel booking environment



\---



\## Key Concepts Applied

\- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

\- Least Privilege

\- Identity-first security

\- Multi-tenant architecture



\---



\## Business Impact

This implementation secures access to booking channels, reduces risk from shared accounts, and establishes a foundation for future security controls such as Conditional Access and BYOD protection.



\---



\## Final Reflection

The identity foundation demonstrates that securing identity first enables all future security layers, including access control, monitoring, and data protection.
