\# Design Principles — PIM \& Privileged Access



\## Overview

This implementation follows Zero Trust and least privilege principles to reduce the exposure of privileged accounts.



\## Principles Applied

\- Least privilege access

\- Just-in-Time administration

\- MFA enforcement

\- Time-bound privileged access

\- Privileged access governance



\## Outcome

Administrative access is controlled, temporary, and monitored to reduce security risk.
