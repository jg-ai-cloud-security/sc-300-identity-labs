\# Business Case — Privileged Access Security



\## Problem

Permanent administrator access creates significant security risk.



\## Solution

Implement Privileged Identity Management (PIM) using eligible role assignments and Just-in-Time activation.



\## Constraints

Due to Microsoft Entra ID P2 licensing limitations, PIM functionality could not be fully activated within the tenant.



The implementation was documented as an architectural and governance design aligned with enterprise privileged access management best practices.



\## Outcome

Reduced exposure of privileged accounts and improved administrative governance.
