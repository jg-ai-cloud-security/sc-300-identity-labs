\# Business Case — Identity Monitoring \& Threat Detection



\## Problem

Suspicious authentication activity may go undetected without proper identity monitoring visibility.



\## Solution

Implement authentication monitoring and investigation processes using Microsoft Entra sign-in visibility and threat detection workflows.



\## Constraints

Some advanced Microsoft Entra Identity Protection capabilities may require additional licensing.



\## Outcome

Improved authentication visibility and stronger identity monitoring capability within the DR Taxi environment.
