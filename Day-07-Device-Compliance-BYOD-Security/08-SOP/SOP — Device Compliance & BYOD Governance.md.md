\# SOP — Device Compliance \& BYOD Governance



\## Device Compliance Review Process



\- Review device compliance visibility

\- Validate Conditional Access controls

\- Confirm endpoint governance requirements

\- Review secure resource access restrictions



\## BYOD Governance



\- Review personal device access controls

\- Validate endpoint compliance readiness

\- Confirm identity protection requirements



\## Purpose



Improve endpoint governance and strengthen secure access controls within the DR Taxi environment.
