\# Business Case — Device Compliance \& BYOD Security



\## Problem



Unmanaged or non-compliant personal devices may increase the risk of data exposure and identity compromise.



\## Solution



Implement Microsoft Intune device compliance and Conditional Access security controls to strengthen secure resource access and endpoint governance.



\## Constraints



Some advanced endpoint protection and mobile device management capabilities may require additional Microsoft licensing depending on business requirements.



\## Outcome



Improved endpoint governance, stronger device compliance visibility, and enhanced secure resource access controls.
