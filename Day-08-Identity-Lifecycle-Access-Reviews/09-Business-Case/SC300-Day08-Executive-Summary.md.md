\# Executive Summary — Identity Lifecycle Governance \& Access Reviews



\## Overview



This implementation focused on reviewing and documenting identity governance, RBAC visibility, and access review controls within the DR Taxi Microsoft Entra ID environment.



The objective was to improve governance visibility, strengthen least privilege access management, and support Zero Trust identity security principles.



\---



\# Technologies Used



\- Microsoft Entra ID

\- Identity Governance

\- RBAC

\- Access Reviews

\- Lifecycle Workflows

\- Microsoft 365 Business Premium



\---



\# Implementation Scope



The implementation included:



\- Reviewing identity governance visibility

\- Reviewing RBAC assignments

\- Reviewing access review governance

\- Reviewing lifecycle governance workflows

\- Validating governance workflow visibility



\---



\# Security Improvements



The implementation improved visibility into:



\- Administrative access governance

\- Least privilege access management

\- User lifecycle governance

\- RBAC visibility

\- Governance workflow awareness



\---



\# Governance Review



During implementation, additional Microsoft Entra ID Governance licensing requirements were identified for advanced governance automation and lifecycle workflow capabilities.



The environment was therefore documented as a governance visibility and architecture assessment aligned with Microsoft 365 Business Premium capabilities.



\---



\# Business Benefits



\- Improved governance visibility

\- Improved RBAC awareness

\- Reduced excessive access risk

\- Improved identity lifecycle awareness

\- Strengthened Zero Trust identity alignment



\---



\# Architecture Outcome



The implementation established a governance-focused identity security architecture supporting:

\- RBAC governance

\- Identity lifecycle visibility

\- Access governance awareness

\- Least privilege access management

\- Identity governance maturity



\---



\# Conclusion



Identity governance and RBAC access reviews strengthen organisational visibility into user access management and support enterprise identity protection strategies aligned with Zero Trust security principles.
