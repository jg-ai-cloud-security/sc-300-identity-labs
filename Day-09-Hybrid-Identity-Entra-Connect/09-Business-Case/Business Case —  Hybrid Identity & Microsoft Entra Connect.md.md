\# Business Case — Hybrid Identity Governance



\## Problem



Without hybrid identity governance and synchronisation visibility, organisations may experience identity inconsistencies, reduced governance visibility, and increased security risk across hybrid environments.



\## Solution



Review Microsoft Entra Connect synchronisation visibility and hybrid identity governance controls to strengthen identity integration awareness and governance planning.



\## Constraints



Some advanced synchronisation and identity governance capabilities require Microsoft Entra ID P1/P2 licensing depending on business requirements.



The implementation was therefore documented as a hybrid identity governance and synchronisation visibility review aligned with Microsoft 365 Business Premium capabilities.



\## Outcome



Improved hybrid identity awareness, synchronisation governance visibility, and identity integration planning within the DR Taxi environment.
