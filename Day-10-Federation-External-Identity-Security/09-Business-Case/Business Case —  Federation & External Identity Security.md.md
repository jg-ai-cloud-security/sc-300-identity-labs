\# Business Case — Federation \& External Identity Security



\## Problem



Without external identity governance and federation security controls, organisations may expose resources to unauthorised guest access, insecure collaboration, and increased identity risk.



\## Solution



Review and document Microsoft Entra ID federation governance, cross-tenant access settings, and B2B collaboration controls to strengthen external identity governance visibility.



\## Constraints



Some advanced federation and cross-tenant governance capabilities may require additional Microsoft Entra licensing depending on organisational requirements.



The implementation was therefore documented using Microsoft 365 Business Premium federation visibility capabilities.



\## Outcome



Improved federation governance awareness, strengthened external identity visibility, and improved secure collaboration planning within the DR Taxi environment.
