\# Business Case — Identity Protection \& Risk-Based Conditional Access



\## Problem



Without identity protection visibility and adaptive Conditional Access governance, compromised accounts and suspicious sign-ins may increase organisational security risk.



\## Solution



Review identity protection visibility and risk-based Conditional Access governance controls to strengthen identity risk awareness and adaptive access planning.



\## Constraints



Some advanced identity protection and adaptive Conditional Access capabilities may require Microsoft Entra ID P2 licensing depending on business requirements.



\## Outcome



Improved identity protection awareness and stronger adaptive access governance visibility within the DR Taxi environment.
