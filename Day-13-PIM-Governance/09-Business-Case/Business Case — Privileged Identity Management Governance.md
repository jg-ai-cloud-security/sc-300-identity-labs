\# Business Case — Privileged Identity Management Governance



\## Business Scenario



DR Taxi requires secure privileged access governance to reduce the risk of excessive administrative permissions and privileged account compromise.



The organisation requires visibility into privileged role assignments, MFA governance, approval workflows, and privileged access monitoring to strengthen Zero Trust identity governance.



\## Business Objective



Implement privileged access governance visibility and strengthen secure administrative access controls using Microsoft Entra ID Privileged Identity Management (PIM).



\## Expected Security Benefits



\- Reduced standing administrative access

\- Improved privileged role governance

\- Improved MFA governance

\- Improved audit visibility

\- Strengthened Zero Trust identity governance
