\# Executive Summary — SC-300 Day 13



This implementation reviewed Privileged Identity Management (PIM) governance within the DR Taxi environment using Microsoft Entra ID.



The implementation focused on:

\- Privileged access governance

\- Eligible role assignments

\- Just-In-Time access

\- MFA enforcement

\- Approval workflow governance

\- Audit visibility

\- Zero Trust privileged access controls



The review improved privileged access governance visibility and strengthened administrative identity security awareness.
