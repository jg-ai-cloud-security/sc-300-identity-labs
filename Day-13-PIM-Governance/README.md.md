\# SC-300 Day 14 — Identity Governance



\## Overview



This implementation reviewed Identity Governance visibility and privileged access governance within Microsoft Entra ID for the DR Taxi environment.



The implementation focused on:

\- Privileged Access Groups (PAG)

\- Access Reviews

\- Administrative Units

\- Entitlement Management

\- Identity lifecycle governance

\- Zero Trust governance controls



\---



\# Business Scenario



DR Taxi requires stronger governance controls over privileged group access, lifecycle governance, and administrative delegation to reduce excessive permissions and improve operational accountability.

\# Business Risk



Without governance visibility and lifecycle validation:

\- Excessive permissions may persist

\- Orphaned accounts may remain active

\- Administrative sprawl may increase

\- Governance accountability may decrease



\---



\# Zero Trust Alignment



\- Verify explicitly

\- Use least privilege access

\- Assume breach



\---



\# Operational Recommendations



\- Review privileged groups monthly

\- Review access certifications regularly

\- Review lifecycle governance quarterly

\- Improve governance audit visibility

\- Expand governance automation where possible
