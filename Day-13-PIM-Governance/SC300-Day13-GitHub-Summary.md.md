\# SC-300 Day 13 — Privileged Identity Management Governance



\## Overview



This implementation reviewed Privileged Identity Management (PIM) governance visibility and secure privileged access controls within the DR Taxi environment using Microsoft Entra ID.



The implementation focused on:

\- Privileged Identity Management (PIM)

\- Just-In-Time (JIT) access

\- Eligible assignments

\- MFA enforcement

\- Approval workflows

\- Audit logging

\- Administrative governance

\- Zero Trust identity governance



\---



\# Business Scenario



DR Taxi requires secure privileged access governance and administrative role protection to reduce standing privileged access exposure and strengthen Zero Trust identity security.



\---



\# Technologies Reviewed



\- Microsoft Entra ID

\- Privileged Identity Management (PIM)

\- MFA

\- RBAC Governance

\- Audit Logging

\- Zero Trust Governance



\---



\# Security Objectives



\- Reduce standing administrative access

\- Improve privileged governance visibility

\- Strengthen administrative accountability

\- Improve audit visibility

\- Strengthen Zero Trust identity governance



\---



\# Implementation Summary



The implementation reviewed:

\- PIM governance overview

\- Eligible role assignments

\- JIT role activation

\- MFA enforcement

\- Audit logging visibility



The implementation validated privileged access governance visibility within the DR Taxi environment.



\---



\# Security Benefits



\- Improved privileged access governance

\- Reduced excessive administrative permissions

\- Improved audit visibility

\- Improved least privilege governance

\- Strengthened Zero Trust identity governance



\---



\# Business Risk



Standing administrative access and excessive privileged permissions may increase identity attack surface exposure and administrative compromise risk.



\---



\# Recommendation



Implement PIM governance, MFA enforcement, approval workflows, and audit visibility to strengthen privileged access governance and reduce excessive permissions exposure.



\---



\# Zero Trust Alignment



\- Verify explicitly

\- Use least privilege access

\- Assume breach



\---



\# Operational Recommendations



\- Review privileged assignments monthly

\- Review audit logs regularly

\- Enforce MFA for privileged activations

\- Review approval workflows periodically

\- Review governance visibility quarterly



\---



\# Outcome



The implementation strengthened privileged access governance and improved administrative security visibility within the DR Taxi environment.
