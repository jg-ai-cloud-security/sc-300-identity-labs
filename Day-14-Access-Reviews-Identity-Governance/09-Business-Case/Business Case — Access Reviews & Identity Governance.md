\# Business Case — Access Reviews \& Identity Governance



\## Problem



Unreviewed user access and unmanaged guest accounts may increase the risk of excessive permissions and unauthorised access exposure.



\## Solution



Review identity governance visibility and access review controls to strengthen lifecycle governance and secure access management.



\## Constraints



Some advanced Identity Governance capabilities may require Microsoft Entra ID P2 licensing.



\## Outcome



Improved governance visibility and strengthened lifecycle governance awareness within the DR Taxi environment.
