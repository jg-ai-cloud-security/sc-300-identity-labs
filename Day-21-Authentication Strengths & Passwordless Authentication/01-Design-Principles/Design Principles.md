\# Day 21 – Authentication Strengths \& Passwordless Authentication



\## Design Principles



\### Zero Trust Security

Never trust, always verify.



\### Passwordless Security

Eliminate password dependency and reduce credential theft.



\### Strong Authentication

Use authentication methods that provide high identity assurance.



\### Phishing Resistance

Protect identities against phishing and credential harvesting attacks.



\### Continuous Identity Verification

Continuously evaluate risk and authentication throughout the session.
