\## Evidence 1 – Authentication Methods Policy



The Authentication Methods Policy was reviewed within Microsoft Entra ID.



The tenant supports multiple authentication methods including:



\- Microsoft Authenticator

\- Passkey (FIDO2)

\- Temporary Access Pass

\- SMS Authentication

\- OATH Tokens

\- Email OTP



These methods provide the foundation for Passwordless Authentication and Authentication Strengths used throughout the organization.
