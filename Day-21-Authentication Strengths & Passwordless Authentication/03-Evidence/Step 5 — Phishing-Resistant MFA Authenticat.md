\# Evidence 5 – Phishing-Resistant MFA Authentication Strength



\## Purpose



Review the built-in Phishing-Resistant MFA Authentication Strength available within Microsoft Entra ID.



\## Findings



The Phishing-Resistant MFA Authentication Strength was reviewed within Microsoft Entra ID Authentication Methods.



This Authentication Strength provides the highest level of authentication assurance and is specifically designed to protect against phishing attacks and credential theft.



The following authentication methods were available:



\- Windows Hello for Business

\- Passkeys (FIDO2)

\- Certificate-Based Authentication (Multifactor)



\## Security Benefits



\- Highest authentication assurance level

\- Resistant to phishing attacks

\- Strong cryptographic authentication

\- Reduces credential theft risks

\- Supports Zero Trust Security



\## Business Impact



Organizations can significantly reduce account compromise risks by requiring phishing-resistant authentication methods for privileged users and critical business applications.



\## Outcome



Phishing-Resistant MFA provides the strongest authentication controls available within Microsoft Entra ID and is recommended for administrative and high-risk accounts.
