\# SC300 Day 01 — AI Enhancements (Identity Security)



\## Overview

AI can improve identity security by detecting abnormal behaviour and risky access patterns.



\---



\## Use Cases



\### 1. Suspicious Login Detection

AI can identify:

\- Unusual login locations

\- Multiple failed login attempts



\---



\### 2. Identity Risk Detection

AI can:

\- Flag compromised accounts

\- Detect unusual activity patterns



\---



\### 3. Access Insights

AI can highlight:

\- Unused accounts

\- Potential over-permissioned users



\---



\## Security Benefit



\- Faster detection of identity threats

\- Improved visibility into user behaviour

\- Reduced risk of unauthorised access



\---



\## Key Takeaway

AI enhances identity security by providing intelligent detection and monitoring.
