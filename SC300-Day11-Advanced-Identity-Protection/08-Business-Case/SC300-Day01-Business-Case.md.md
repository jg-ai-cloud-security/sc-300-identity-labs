\# SC300 Day 01 — Business Case



\## Scenario



DR Taxi operates booking, customer communication, and payment systems that require secure user access.



\---



\## Problem



Access to systems is not centrally managed, leading to inconsistent permissions across users.



\---



\## Risk



Unauthorised access to booking data or financial information, potentially causing data exposure and operational disruption.





\---



\## Solution



Implement Microsoft Entra ID with RBAC to centralise and control access.



\---



\## Goal



Ensure secure, role-based identity access aligned with Zero Trust principles.



\---



\## Recommendation



Use least privilege and group-based access control, with regular access reviews.
